## Work Flows

Noodlestan is built in the open, one small piece at a time, with many larger efforts moving together in clunky and discontinuous ways.

The projects live in separate repositories and are coordinated through a shared workspace. Each repository can be built and used on its own.

Work is organised through roadmaps, milestones, plans, and iterations. Plans describe the work, its scope, commits, and how completion will be verified. Roadmaps and milestones coordinate priorities and dependencies.

Projects also carry structured records (Art) describing what they are and how they relate to the rest of the ecosystem. These records are used as sources of truth by agents as well as by tools.

### Artificial Work

AI agents are used extensively for planning, drafting, coding, documentation, and automating parts of the work. Since part of the work is to make everything artificial more effective I have been able to keep the bill under twenty bucks a month.

The motivation behind many of these projects has often been to make work flow better. More recently, it has also become about making artificial work more effective and sustainable, by continuously designing and calibrating its processes, turning what works into reusable knowledge, tools, and infrastructure.

Noodlestan operates under the principle that for every artificial process, there should be a plan to decompose it and extract every deterministic operation into something that can be made explicit, reproducible, and independent of the agent performing it.

The tools, conventions, and ways of working are themselves part of the experiment. The AGENTS, skills, and other context files — a lot of MD and a little Art here and there — are not public yet. The goal is to distribute them in different formats for use on different platforms, without Noodlestan dependencies.
