## Hero

Writing software because. Just because.

A home for software projects, experiments, ideas, and things worth building.

## Projects

Tools for working with code and metadata, component libraries and design systems, AI agent tooling, documentation infrastructure, and creative experiments.

The projects are independent, but many are connected and built to work together.

## Work Flows

Noodlestan is built in the open, one small piece at a time.

The projects live in separate repositories and are coordinated through a shared workspace. Each repository can be built and used on its own.

Work is organised through roadmaps, milestones, plans, and iterations. Plans describe the work, its scope, commits, and how completion will be verified.

Projects also carry structured records describing what they are and how they relate to the rest of the ecosystem. These records are used as sources of information for tools and websites, including this one.

AI agents are used extensively for planning, drafting, coding, documentation, and other parts of the work. Their roles, instructions, and skills are written down and published alongside the projects.

The tools, conventions, and ways of working are themselves part of the experiment.
